Cure order

  1. Pull the tax and water balances on OPA 622239900 before any money moves. Two phone calls, 20 minutes, and they are the only claims that survive closing as a surprise.
  2. Read every notice in the table. Each carries the exact action the inspector ordered, and each PDF is linked above for the file.
  3. File the permits for the cited conditions in one package with the district office at 8716 Frankford Ave, Philadelphia PA 19136, 215-683-2547. Unpermitted cure work is the most common way a violation reopens.
  4. Correct, then request reinspection at (215) 255-4040 and quote the case number. The case closes when the inspector signs off and the fee balance is paid.
